## 1.0.0

- Initial release.
- Dashboard with a combined summary heatmap and per-habit heatmaps (last 52 weeks).
- Daily log screen to check off today's habits.
- Manage screen to add and delete habits, persisted immediately.
- Local JSON storage under `~/.habit_cli` (macOS/Linux) or `%APPDATA%\habit_cli` (Windows).
- Distributed via Homebrew (macOS) and winget (Windows).
